## Publication Rules

1. Add an article only after both its caption and final still are explicitly approved.
2. Use the exact approved asset at its native aspect ratio; do not crop it into a generic card ratio.
3. Store no engagement metric or LinkedIn URL until the real value is known.
4. Treat motion as an optional companion to the canonical still.
5. Give every article the same useful depth: framework, required inputs, review questions, and decision boundary.
6. Record the source week so future audits can trace the website entry back to the content engine.
